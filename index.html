05 Insights & Commentary Occasional writing — deliberately so.

Notes from the practice.

Ninety percent of trade-secret matters are effectively decided in the first six weeks, long before discovery matures. A well-timed injunction does not merely stop the bleeding — it re-prices the entire settlement conversation, because the other side’s business plan now depends on a remedy only you can concede.

Most succession plans fail for reasons that have nothing to do with law — and almost everything to do with sequencing. This paper sets out the five structural decisions that determine whether the second generation keeps the enterprise, or merely its proceeds.

The standard arbitration clause is a bet on process; the good one is a bet on outcome. The difference is usually nine words or fewer — most often concerning interim relief, and who pays for the privilege of being wrong.

Legislative timelines are not planning deadlines — but they are pricing signals. This briefing models three exemption scenarios and identifies the transfers that remain efficient under every one of them, and the single structure that only works if executed early.

Full memoranda are provided to clients of the firm and, on request, under a mutual confidentiality understanding.
